# Periodic Orbits Around a Spherically Symmetric Naked Singularity

> A paper-reading and AI-assisted reproduction project based on  
> **G. Z. Babar, A. Z. Babar, and Y.-K. Lim, Phys. Rev. D 96, 084052 (2017).**

## Paper

**Title:** Periodic orbits around a spherically symmetric naked singularity

**Authors:** Gulmina Zaman Babar, Adil Zaman Babar, Yen-Kheng Lim

**Journal:** Physical Review D, 96, 084052 (2017)

**arXiv:** [1710.09581](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.09581)

**DOI:** [10.1103/PhysRevD.96.084052](https://doi.org/10.1103/PhysRevD.96.084052)

## Overview

This project is a study and partial reproduction of the paper
*Periodic orbits around a spherically symmetric naked singularity*.

The paper investigates timelike test-particle motion in the
Fisher/Janis-Newman-Winicour (F/JNW) spacetime, a spherically symmetric
solution involving a massless scalar field.

The main topics include geodesic motion, the effective potential,
circular orbits, and periodic orbits.

---

## Objectives

The project aims to:

- Understand the physical and mathematical setup of the F/JNW spacetime.
- Reconstruct the geodesic equations using the Hamiltonian formulation.
- Understand the effective potential for timelike particles.
- Study circular orbits and their stability.
- Understand the conditions for periodic orbits.
- Reproduce selected analytical and numerical results from the paper.
- Use computational tools to verify mathematical derivations and explore the results.

---

## Reproduction Approach

The reproduction follows the main structure of the paper.

### 1. Geodesic Motion

Study the F/JNW metric and reconstruct the equations of motion using the
Hamiltonian formulation.

This includes the conserved energy, angular momentum, and radial motion
of timelike test particles.

### 2. Effective Potential

Derive and analyze the effective potential governing radial motion.

Particular attention is given to:

- Turning points
- Bound orbits
- Angular momentum dependence
- The scalar-field parameter
- The behavior near the naked singularity

### 3. Circular Orbits

Study the conditions for circular orbits and their stability, including
the innermost bound and innermost stable circular orbits.

### 4. Periodic Orbits

Investigate the conditions under which bound particle trajectories become
periodic.

The paper considers periodic orbits associated with rational orbital
frequencies. Representative trajectories are reproduced numerically.

## AI-Assisted Research

AI tools were used as part of the research workflow, particularly for:

- Interpreting difficult sections of the paper
- Reconstructing intermediate mathematical steps
- Checking algebraic manipulations
- Translating equations into computational procedures
- Debugging numerical calculations
- Exploring alternative derivations

AI-generated results were not treated as authoritative. Mathematical
derivations and numerical results were checked against the original paper
and the underlying mathematical and physical definitions.

The purpose of this project is to explore how AI can be used as a
research assistant while retaining independent verification and
understanding of the underlying mathematics and physics.
